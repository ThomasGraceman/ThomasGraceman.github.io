This book by Jiri Matousek is an introduction to discrete and combinatorial geometry. It covers convexity, lattices and the geometry of numbers, arrangements of hyperplanes, hypergraphs and combinatorial topology, along with classical results such as those of Helly, Ramsey, and Borsuk. A standard reference for the area.
